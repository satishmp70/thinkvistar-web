DATA GOVERNANCE

Privacy Policy

How Thinkvistar LLP collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you visit our website, contact us, or use the services and platforms we provide.

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Thinkvistar LLP (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses, stores and discloses information, including personal information (“Information”), when you visit this website (the “Site”), request a proposal or use the project estimator, contact our team by email, phone or WhatsApp, or use software or services we deliver (the “Services”). Unless a Service Agreement signed with you says otherwise, the Information we collect or receive is used and protected as set out below. By using the Site you acknowledge this Policy. Where we need your consent (for example, to send you marketing), we ask for it separately and you may withdraw it at any time. We use the details you send in an enquiry in order to respond to you; during an engagement we process information to perform the contract and to meet legal obligations.

2. Personal Information We Collect

Unless otherwise stated in a specific Service Agreement, the personal information collected during registration, while filling in contact or enquiry forms, or during the course of an engagement shall constitute your Personal Information. This may include:

  • Identity and contact details: first name, last name, company or organization name, designation, email address, contact number, postal address, city, PIN/ZIP code, and country.
  • Business and project details: project requirements, technical specifications, budget ranges, timelines, and any documents or credentials you choose to share for the purpose of delivering the Services.
  • Account information: usernames, encrypted passwords, roles, and activity logs where we provide you with a dashboard, admin panel, or API access.
  • Billing information: GSTIN, billing address, and payment references. We do not store full card or bank details; payments are processed by third-party payment gateways.
  • Communication records: emails, WhatsApp messages, call notes, and support tickets exchanged with our team.

3. Non-Personal & Technical Information

When you visit the Site, our website host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, pages requested and time) for security and troubleshooting. To limit spam, the enquiry form keeps, for up to a few hours, a record that contains a one-way hash of your network address and the time of your request. The Site does not currently use analytics, advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. We use such technical information to keep the Site working and secure; we do not use it to build a profile of you.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the Information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiries, prepare technical proposals and estimates, and coordinate consultations.
  • To deliver, operate, maintain, and support the Services and platforms we build for you, including creating and managing accounts.
  • To issue invoices, process payments, and maintain accounting and tax records as required by Indian law.
  • To communicate project updates, milestone approvals, security notices, and changes to our terms or policies.
  • To send occasional information about our services, case studies or offers, only if you have chosen to receive it; you may unsubscribe at any time.
  • To detect, prevent, and address fraud, security incidents, abuse, or violations of our Terms and Conditions.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We never sell, rent, or trade your Personal Information to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

5. Sharing & Disclosure of Information

We do not share your Personal Information with third parties without your permission, except in the following cases:

  • With our team members and contractors who need the information to deliver the Services and who are bound to keep it confidential and to protect it in line with this Policy.
  • With service providers that help us run our business and deliver the Services, such as website hosting, email, messaging and cloud providers and, where relevant, payment gateways, who may use the information only for the purposes we specify and are required to protect it. If you contact us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also handled by that platform under its own terms.
  • With courts, regulators, law enforcement or government authorities where required by law, court order or legal process, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud or a security incident.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, restructuring or sale of all or part of our business, in which case the recipient will be required to protect the Information consistently with this Policy.
  • In aggregated or anonymised form that cannot reasonably be used to identify you, for example in portfolio case studies or performance statistics.

6. Client Data & End-User Information

When we build, host or maintain a platform on your behalf, we may process data belonging to your customers or end users (“Client Data”). In that case you are the controller (the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) and we act as your processor. We process Client Data only on your documented instructions and as set out in our service terms or a data processing agreement; we do not use it for our own purposes, and we delete or return it at the end of the engagement unless the law requires us to keep it. You are responsible for obtaining the consents and giving the notices that apply to your own users, and for ensuring your own privacy notices cover the platforms we build for you.

7. Cookies & Analytics

The Site does not use cookies for analytics or advertising and does not currently run analytics or cross-site tracking. If we add privacy-focused analytics in future, we will update this Policy first. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your Information, appropriate to the risk. These include encrypted connections (TLS) for this Site and for data in transit, limiting access to personal information to people who need it for their work, and keeping credentials and secrets out of public source code. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your Personal Information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention

We keep Personal Information only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to deliver and support the Services, and to meet legal, accounting and tax obligations. We aim to delete enquiry data that does not lead to an engagement within twenty-four (24) months. Project and billing records are kept for the period that applicable law requires (for financial records this is typically up to eight years). Client Data is handled as described in Section 6.

10. Your Rights & Choices

You have rights over your Personal Information under applicable law. The rights in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 apply from the date the relevant provisions and the Rules made under it come into force (for most obligations this is currently expected to be 13 May 2027). Until then, and afterwards, we will respond to reasonable requests about your Personal Information. Subject to applicable law, you may:

  • Access the Personal Information we hold about you and request a copy.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Information.
  • Request deletion of your Personal Information, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India or other competent authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to hello@thinkvistar.com. We will reply within a reasonable time and in any event within the period the law requires.

11. Third-Party Links & Services

The Site and Services may contain links to third-party websites and services (such as LinkedIn, WhatsApp or payment and cloud platforms). We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content, and this Policy does not apply to them. We encourage you to read their policies.

12. Children's Privacy

Our Site and Services are intended for businesses and professionals and are not directed at children under eighteen (18) years of age. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with Personal Information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. International Data Transfers

Thinkvistar LLP is based in India. Depending on the hosting, email and communication providers used for your project, Information may be stored or processed on servers outside India. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected consistently with this Policy and applicable law, and project-specific locations are agreed in the SOW or a data processing agreement.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. Material changes affecting active clients will be communicated by email. Your continued use of the Site or Services after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

15. Grievance Officer & Contact

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under it, if you have questions, concerns or complaints about this Policy or the handling of your Personal Information, contact the Grievance Officer of Thinkvistar LLP at hello@thinkvistar.com, or write to Thinkvistar LLP, Yashwant Gaurav Phase 1, Nalasopara West, Maharashtra, India. We aim to acknowledge grievances within two (2) working days and to resolve them within thirty (30) days, or within the period the law requires. If your grievance is not resolved you may approach the Data Protection Board of India or another competent authority, as the law allows.
