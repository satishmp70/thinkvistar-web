What affects the cost of a software project?

  • Scope

    The number of user roles, screens and features in the first release.

  • Integrations

    Payment gateways, ERPs, CRMs, messaging (SMS, email, WhatsApp) and other third-party APIs.

  • Data migration

    Moving records or content from an existing system without losing anything.

  • Security & compliance

    Requirements such as India’s DPDP Act, HIPAA for US healthcare data, or audit logging.

  • Timeline

    A fast-track launch needs a larger team working in parallel.

Third-party costs such as cloud hosting, domains and paid APIs are billed at actual cost, and fees are exclusive of GST. Full details are in our terms & conditions.

FAQ

Questions about working with us

Which engagement model is right for my project?

Choose a fixed-scope project when requirements are clear and you want a fixed set of deliverables; a dedicated engineering pod when your roadmap is evolving and you want senior engineers inside your sprints; or an architecture review when you need an expert assessment of existing code, security or scalability before committing to a larger build.

Will you sign an NDA before we share our idea?

Yes. We are happy to sign your NDA (or provide ours) before a detailed discussion. Our terms also commit both parties to confidentiality for three years after an engagement, and indefinitely for trade secrets and personal data.

Can you take over or improve an existing codebase?

Yes. We start with a short code and infrastructure review, document the risks we find, and then either stabilise and extend the existing system or plan a staged modernization — without a risky big-bang rewrite.

What happens after launch?

Every release includes a 30-day warranty during which we fix reproducible defects at no extra cost. After that, you can move to a support plan covering monitoring, security patching, backups and ongoing improvements — see Cloud & 24/7 Support.

Which technologies do you work with?

Mainly TypeScript, React and Next.js on the front end; Node.js, Python (FastAPI) and Go on the back end; PostgreSQL, Redis and MongoDB for data; and AWS, Vercel, Cloudflare and Docker for hosting. See the full technology stack and how we choose it.
