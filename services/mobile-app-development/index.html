FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Should I build a native app or use React Native / Flutter?

For most business apps, a cross-platform framework (React Native or Flutter) gives you iOS and Android from one codebase at a lower cost, with near-native performance. We recommend fully native development only for apps with heavy graphics, advanced hardware access or platform-specific features.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

A focused first release on both stores usually takes 6–10 weeks, including design, development, testing and store review. Apps with complex integrations or offline sync take longer.

Do you publish the app to the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare the store listings, screenshots and privacy disclosures, and handle submission and review. The apps are published under your own developer accounts, so you own them.

Can you build an app for my existing website or platform?

Yes. We reuse your existing backend where possible, add the APIs the app needs, and keep user accounts shared between web and mobile.
