FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a SaaS MVP?

A focused MVP usually takes 4–6 weeks: sign-up and onboarding, the core workflow, basic roles and subscription billing. Larger platforms are delivered in phases. Our estimator gives a timeline range for your scope.

Should my SaaS be multi-tenant from day one?

Almost always, yes. Designing tenant isolation into the data model from the start is far cheaper than retrofitting it after you have customers. We choose between shared-schema and schema-per-tenant based on your compliance and scale needs.

Which payment gateways do you integrate?

Stripe and Paddle for international customers, and Razorpay or Cashfree for Indian customers — including subscriptions, trials, coupons, GST invoices and failed-payment retries.

Do I own the code?

Yes. Once the agreed payment is made, the source code, designs and database schemas built for you are yours, and we hand over repositories and documentation. See our terms.
