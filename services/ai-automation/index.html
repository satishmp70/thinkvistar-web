FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is our data safe when using AI models?

We choose provider plans and settings under which, according to the provider’s current published terms, your data is not used to train models; we restrict what is sent to the model and keep documents in your own infrastructure. Provider terms change, so we confirm them for your project. For sensitive data we can use self-hosted open models.

How accurate is AI document extraction?

It depends on document quality and variety. We measure accuracy on your real documents during the prototype, and add human review for low-confidence results so errors are caught before they reach your systems.

Which AI models do you work with?

Hosted models such as OpenAI GPT, Anthropic Claude and Google Gemini through their APIs, and open models when data must stay on your servers. We choose based on accuracy, cost and privacy requirements.

Where should a small business start with AI automation?

Start with one repetitive, document-heavy task where time spent is easy to measure — such as processing invoices or answering common questions — prove it works, then expand.
