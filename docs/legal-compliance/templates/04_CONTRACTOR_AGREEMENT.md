# Contractor / Developer Agreement (IP assignment, confidentiality, data protection) — starter template

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL. Not legal advice.** Use for every freelancer or contractor who writes code, designs or sees client data. Partners and employees need equivalent clauses in the LLP Agreement / employment terms. Without this, a contractor may be the first owner of what they create (Copyright Act, 1957, ss.17–19; counsel to confirm).

**Date:** [●]  **Between:** Thinkvistar LLP, [address] and [Contractor name, address, PAN/GSTIN if any].

1. **Services.** The Contractor will provide [description] on the terms of each work order (scope, fee, dates) issued by Thinkvistar.
2. **Independent contractor.** No employment, partnership or agency; the Contractor is responsible for its own taxes and equipment. [Counsel: classification risk.]
3. **Intellectual property.** All work product the Contractor creates in the course of the Services (code, designs, documentation and related rights) is assigned to Thinkvistar LLP, worldwide, for the full term of the rights, effective on creation [and in any case on payment]; the Contractor will sign any confirmatory assignment requested. The Contractor keeps its pre-existing tools and grants Thinkvistar and its clients a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use any that are embedded in the work product. [Moral rights — counsel.]
4. **Originality and licences.** The Contractor will not include code, text, images or fonts it has no right to use, will not copy from other clients or employers, and will list any third-party or open-source component with its licence before including it. Copyleft licences require Thinkvistar's prior approval.
5. **Confidentiality.** The Contractor will keep Thinkvistar's and its clients' Confidential Information secret using the exceptions and duration in Terms section 12 (during the work and for [three] years afterwards; trade secrets and personal data for as long as they remain protected).
6. **Client data and security.** Access only as needed; use company-approved accounts; multi-factor authentication where available; no copying of client data to personal devices or services; no sharing credentials; report suspected incidents within [24] hours; return or delete all materials on request or at the end.
7. **No conflicts; no re-use.** The Contractor will not reuse or sell work product, or use client information for any other purpose, and will disclose any conflict of interest.
8. **Fees and payment.** As in each work order; payment on acceptance of the work unless stated otherwise.
9. **Term and termination.** Either party may end a work order on [14] days' notice; Thinkvistar may terminate immediately for breach of sections 3–7.
10. **Liability and indemnity.** The Contractor indemnifies Thinkvistar against third-party claims arising from its breach of section 4. [Counsel: liability limits.]
11. **Law and disputes.** Indian law; arbitration in Mumbai as in Terms section 22.
