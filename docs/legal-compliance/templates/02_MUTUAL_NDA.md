# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — starter template

**Date:** [●]  **Between:** Thinkvistar LLP, [address] ("Thinkvistar") and [Counterparty name, address] ("Counterparty"), each a "Party".

**1. Purpose.** The Parties wish to discuss [a possible software development engagement] (the "Purpose") and may disclose Confidential Information to each other for that Purpose only.

**2. Confidential Information** means non-public information disclosed by a Party (the "Discloser") to the other (the "Recipient") in any form that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential, including business plans, customer and personal data, credentials, source code, designs, specifications and the existence and terms of discussions.

**3. Obligations.** The Recipient will (a) use Confidential Information only for the Purpose; (b) protect it with at least reasonable care; (c) disclose it only to its employees, contractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent confidentiality duties; and (d) not copy it except as needed for the Purpose.

**4. Exceptions.** These duties do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes public other than through breach; (b) the Recipient already knew free of any duty; (c) the Recipient lawfully receives from a third party; (d) the Recipient develops independently without using it; or (e) must be disclosed by law, court order or regulator — in which case the Recipient will, where lawful, give prompt notice and disclose only what is required.

**5. Return and deletion.** On request or when discussions end, the Recipient will return or delete Confidential Information, except copies required by law or held in routine backups, which stay subject to this Agreement.

**6. No licence; no obligation.** No rights in Confidential Information or intellectual property are granted. Neither Party is obliged to enter into any further agreement, and each may develop independent products and services, provided it does not use the other's Confidential Information.

**7. Personal data.** Each Party will handle any personal data disclosed in accordance with applicable data-protection law.

**8. Term.** Obligations apply to information disclosed during [two (2)] years from the date above and continue for three (3) years after the end of that period, and for as long as trade secrets remain secret and personal data remains held.

**9. Remedies.** A breach may cause harm for which damages are inadequate; the Discloser may seek injunctive relief from a competent court, in addition to other remedies.

**10. General.** Indian law governs. Disputes: escalation, then arbitration in Mumbai under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (sole arbitrator, English), as in Terms section 22; interim relief from courts is not excluded. Entire agreement on this subject; amendments in writing; counterparts and electronic signature are valid. [Counsel: add any stamp duty requirement for the relevant state.]
