# Intellectual Property Assignment Deed — starter template

> **DRAFT FOR COUNSEL. Not legal advice.** Counsel must confirm the form, any stamp duty, and whether registration or recordal is advisable. This follows the structure the Copyright Act, 1957 contemplates for an assignment (a written, signed instrument identifying the work, the rights assigned, the territory and the duration — counsel to confirm the section 19 requirements).

**Date:** [●]
**Assignor:** Thinkvistar LLP, LLPIN [●], of [address].
**Assignee:** [Client full legal name], of [address].

**Background.** Under SOW No. [●] dated [●] (the "SOW") Thinkvistar created the Deliverables for the Assignee. The SOW incorporates the Thinkvistar Terms and Conditions (Part B). The Assignee has paid the fees for the Deliverables listed in Schedule 1 in full.

1. **Assignment.** In consideration of the fees paid under the SOW, the Assignor assigns to the Assignee all right, title and interest, including copyright, in the Bespoke Deliverables listed in Schedule 1 (source code, visual assets, database schemas and customised software created specifically for the Assignee), **throughout the world** and **for the full term of the rights**, including the right to reproduce, adapt, distribute, communicate to the public and sublicense.
2. **Exclusions.** The assignment does not include Pre-Existing Materials, Thinkvistar's developer tools and know-how, or Third-Party and open-source components (Schedule 2). The Assignor grants the Assignee the non-exclusive, worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free licence described in Terms section 11 to use the Pre-Existing Materials as embedded in the Bespoke Deliverables.
3. **Effective date.** The assignment takes effect on the date the Assignor receives payment in full of the fees for the relevant Deliverables [or the date of this deed, if later].
4. **Warranties.** The Assignor confirms that it has the right to assign the Bespoke Deliverables and that, to its knowledge, they do not include third-party code or assets other than those in Schedule 2. Liability is subject to Terms sections 17 and 18.
5. **Moral rights; further assurance.** [Counsel: moral rights position.] The Assignor will sign further documents reasonably needed to record or perfect the assignment, at the Assignee's cost.
6. **Governing law and disputes.** As in Terms section 22.
