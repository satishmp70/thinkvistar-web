> **UPDATE 22 Oct 2026 (second pass, on the owner's instruction):** the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy text in `src/site-data.json` **has now been rewritten in the working tree** using the drafts in `PROPOSED_LEGAL_REVISIONS.md`. Sections of this document that describe the Terms or Privacy Policy "as published" describe the text **before** this update; see `IMPLEMENTATION_LOG.md` §5 for exactly what changed. Do not deploy the new text until counsel has reviewed it.

Rating = likelihood × impact, each Low/Medium/High, judged for a small, newly founded (2026) software studio serving domestic and overseas B2B clients. These are judgements, not statistics. "Today" describes what is in the published Terms and the repository now; "Proposed" refers to `PROPOSED_LEGAL_REVISIONS.md` (R-numbers). Residual risk assumes the proposed controls are adopted **and** actually followed in practice. A control that exists only on paper does not reduce risk.

## Reading the register

- **Highest-value actions** (in order): CR-01 (R1/R2), CR-08 (R8 assignment), CR-06/CR-04 (R6, R11), CR-18 (staff and contractor agreements), CR-10 (security programme and incident terms).
- **Cannot be removed by drafting:** the quality of delivery, whether the written scope is actually specific, whether invoices are issued on time, and whether security practice matches what is promised.
- **Insurance:** no evidence of professional indemnity or cyber cover exists in the repository. Speak to a broker; the liability cap in R11 should be chosen together with the cover.
