## How each clause was tested

The ten tests from the review brief, referred to by number in the "Fails" column:

1. Legally permissible  2. Clear and unambiguous  3. Accurately describes the company's real obligations  4. Could a client reasonably misunderstand it? (fail = yes)  5. Could it create an unintended guarantee? (fail = yes)  6. Commercially balanced  7. A court or arbitrator could refuse to enforce it? (fail = yes)  8. Conflicts with another policy or agreement (fail = yes)  9. Needs factual verification (fail = yes)  10. Needs approval from qualified counsel (fail = yes)

Test 10 applies to every clause: nothing here is approved for publication without counsel. The "Fails" column therefore lists tests 1–9 only; read it as "counsel must also approve".

Severity is the combined likelihood and impact of a dispute or loss if the clause stays as written.

## B. Privacy Policy

| § | Clause (what it says) | Problem and exact source | Risk | Sev | Fails | LR | Rev |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction & Scope | "By using the Site or Services, you consent to the practices described." Browsing is not consent that satisfies DPDP's standard (free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous, clear affirmative action) once the core duties start on 13 May 2027, and "consent" is the wrong basis for much processing (contract, legitimate uses). Under the SPDI Rules now, consent in writing is needed to collect *sensitive* data only | Invalid consent basis; regulator complaint after May 2027 | Medium | 1, 7 | LR-12 | R21 |
| 2 | Personal Information We Collect | Accurate list for enquiries, accounts, billing, communications. States "We do not store full card or bank details" and "encrypted passwords": **operational claims requiring verification** | False statement if untrue | Medium | 9 | LR-14 | R21 |
| 3 | Non-Personal & Technical Information | "we and our hosting or analytics providers may automatically collect…IP address, browser…" The site installs **no analytics** (confirmed in code), so mention of analytics providers is inaccurate; hosting-level server logs do exist (host not reviewed). "Not used to identify you" is a strong statement | Inaccurate statement; minor | Low | 3, 9 | LR-23 | R21 |
| 4 | How We Use Your Information | Purposes listed; "We never sell, rent, or trade" is an absolute but verifiable by design. "Where you have opted in" for marketing: **no marketing list or opt-in exists on the site** | Low | Low | 9 | LR-23 | R21 |
| 5 | Sharing & Disclosure | Conditions for sharing without consent; lists affiliated entities (none identified), "strategic relationship" partners, analytics providers (none). Fine in structure; needs a **sub-processor list** | Medium-low | Low | 3, 9 | LR-16 | R15, R21 |
| 6 | Client Data & End-User Information | Client = "data controller", Thinkvistar = "data processor": sound. Under DPDP the terms are Data Fiduciary and Data Processor; a **written processing agreement** is needed (DPDP requires a contract when a fiduciary engages a processor). No instruction, sub-processor, security, audit, incident or deletion terms | Allocation of breach responsibility unclear | Medium | 2, 6, 8 | LR-16 | R15 |
| 7 | Cookies & Analytics | Corrected earlier in this project to say no analytics or advertising cookies; still mentions embedded maps/videos that are not on the site | Low | Low | 3 | LR-23 | R21 |
| 8 | Data Security | Lists TLS, encrypted credential storage, secure hosting, role-based access controls, "strict internal access policies"; acknowledges no method is completely secure and no guarantee: good. "We will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law": CERT-In directions (6-hour reporting) may apply to Thinkvistar itself; applicability not established. **Every listed control must be true** | False-security-claim exposure | Medium | 3, 9 | LR-14, LR-16 | R15, R21 |
| 9 | Data Retention | Enquiries deleted "generally within twenty-four (24) months"; financial records "typically eight years". Needs a retention schedule and a real deletion routine (enquiries sit in a mailbox; the form handler stores only hashed rate-limit counters). | 11 | Third-Party Links | Mentions GitHub, LinkedIn, payment gateways, cloud consoles. Site links to LinkedIn, Facebook, X, Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Maps | Low | Low | — | — | — |

| 13 | International Data Transfers | Honest: may be stored outside India depending on providers; DPDP s.16 generally permits transfers except to countries the government restricts by notification. **Actual hosting/mail locations unknown to this review** | Low-medium | Low | 9 | LR-14 | R15 |
| 14 | Changes to This Privacy Policy | Same unilateral-change structure as Terms §16; acceptable for a privacy notice if material changes are notified | Low | Low | 6 | LR-09 | R21 |
