# Missing agreements and policies

Nothing in the repository shows that any of these exist. Where one exists outside the repository, record it here and review it against `PROPOSED_LEGAL_REVISIONS.md`. Priority: **P1** = needed before the next signed engagement; **P2** = within a quarter; **P3** = as the business grows. Drafts are for counsel to prepare or approve; the R-numbers point to starter wording that is already drafted.

> **Update 22 Oct 2026:** starter drafts now exist in `templates/` for items 2, 3, 4 (SOW with Change Request and Acceptance Certificate annexes), 5 (IP Assignment Deed), 6 (Mutual NDA), 7 (Contractor Agreement) and 17 (Publicity consent). They are drafts for counsel and unsigned. The Master Services Agreement (item 1) is covered by Part B of the published Terms until a separate MSA is prepared.
