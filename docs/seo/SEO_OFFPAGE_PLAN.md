# Off-page plan (execution plan — nothing here has been done)

All white-hat. No purchased links, PBNs, link exchanges, or automated outreach.

## Weeks 1–2: identity and profiles (free, highest impact for brand/AI)
- Google Business Profile (service-area or appointment-only per Google rules; exact footer NAP). Verify by postcard/video.
- LinkedIn company page; fill every field; same name/address/phone; link to site.
- Crunchbase, Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, Sortlist, IndiaMART/Justdial only where relevant. Identical details everywhere.
- X, Instagram, Facebook already linked in schema `sameAs`; keep active or remove.

## Weeks 2–6: earned links and mentions
- Ask the 3 current clients (Viora Healthcare, Viva Homecare, The Walk) for a "built by Thinkvistar" credit link and, if willing, a short testimonial/case study. Real relationships, strongest early links.
- Answer questions on Stack Overflow, Reddit (r/webdev, r/SaaS, r/indiehackers), Indie Hackers, Dev.to, with real help; link only where relevant.
- Guest/expert contributions to Indian startup and dev publications (YourStory, Inc42, DEV, Hashnode) once case studies exist.
- Tool-based link magnet: the free project estimator can be pitched to startup communities.

## Ongoing
- Original data posts (timelines/cost ranges from delivered projects) for journalists.
- Review process: after each delivery, send a Google/Clutch review request (ask, never incentivise; never fake).
- Competitor gap: export referring domains of 3–5 competitors (Ahrefs/Semrush/Bing) and pursue shared, relevant ones.

## Measure
Referring domains (relevant ones), brand searches, citations in AI answers. Do not measure by raw link count.
